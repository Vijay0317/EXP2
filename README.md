# EXP2
## Design and develop a detailed 3D solid model of a piston head that balances strength, weight, and dimensional accuracy. Create a complete piston head assembly with proper constraints, clearances, and alignment to ensure functional fit with the piston pin and connecting rod. Perform simulations to evaluate the structural integrity and durability of the piston head under operating conditions. Finally, prepare the model for prototyping using suitable manufacturing methods and document the complete design, assembly, simulation, and improvement process for presentation.

## DESCRIPTION:

The model represents a drone arm designed using CATIA 3DEXPERIENCE software. It consists of an elongated arm structure with circular holes for motor mounting and component attachment. The model includes a larger circular opening at one end and multiple smaller holes at the other end for fastening purposes. The design was created using sketching, dimensional constraints, and 3D modelling features in the Part Design workbench. It demonstrates the application of CAD techniques in designing lightweight and functional drone components.

## CHALLENGES FACED TO DEVELOP:

The main challenges faced during the development of the drone arm model were maintaining accurate dimensions, positioning the circular holes correctly, and ensuring uniform thickness throughout the structure. Creating holes of different diameters and aligning them properly required careful sketching and the application of geometric constraints. Selecting suitable modelling features and maintaining the correct proportions of the arm also required attention. These challenges were overcome through careful use of CATIA tools and repeated adjustments to achieve the desired geometry.
